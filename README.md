# HUGPONG

### Agricultural Management Platform

HUGPONG is a Web and Android application that helps sugarcane block farms in Silay City, Philippines organize farm activities, monitor expenses, and prepare reports for the Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA).

It brings Farm Members, Farm Managers, and SRA administrators together through a shared record of field activities, making farm information easier to access, review, and understand.

## Our purpose

HUGPONG aims to make everyday farm recording and reporting simpler. By keeping plans, completed activities, costs, and reports in one place, it helps farmers and managers maintain organized records and supports more informed farm decisions.

The platform helps users:

- **Record farm work** and follow activities throughout the crop cycle.
- **Monitor field progress and expenses** to understand how resources are being used.
- **Prepare and review monthly reports** with records that can be traced back to field activities.
- **Access analytics and SRA price references** to support planning and farm supervision.

## Built for the field and the office

The Android app supports field recording, including selected offline activities that synchronize when a connection returns. The online Web console supports farm management, report review, and system administration.

Together, they help keep field work and farm oversight connected.
