Transparent Web Storage Disclosure

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 | Version 2.4

1 What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and Web Storage technologies (such as HTML5 localStorage, sessionStorage, and mobile AsyncStorage) are small text and data files stored on your device to maintain session continuity and store offline operational records.

2 How HUGPONG Uses Local Storage

HUGPONG uses storage technologies exclusively for Strictly Necessary operational purposes:

Key Name Type Purpose Lifespan
hugpong_auth_token Local Storage Cryptographic JWT token ensuring authenticated session state without re-login. 7 Days (Rolling)
hugpong_session Local Storage Cached user profile (Role, Name, Assigned Block Farm) for offline display. Session / 7 Days
hugpong_db Local Storage Offline-first local cache of field plots, crop stages, and pending sync logs. Persistent until Synced
hugpong_cookie_consent Local Storage Records your acknowledgment of our privacy & storage notice. 1 Year

3 Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

HUGPONG does NOT install third-party tracking cookies, marketing beacons, social media retargeting pixels, or commercial tracking scripts. Your agricultural and personal data is never shared with advertiser networks.

4 Managing Your Storage Preferences

You can clear your stored credentials and offline cache at any time via the "Sign Out" button or through your browser's settings (Clear Site Data / Storage). Note that clearing offline storage before synchronizing pending field logs may result in unsaved field operation entries being lost.
