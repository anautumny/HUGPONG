Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 | Last Updated: September 12, 2026 | Version 2.4

1 Statement of Privacy Commitment

HUGPONG Agricultural Management Platform ("HUGPONG", "we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to protecting the privacy, integrity, and confidentiality of the personal and agricultural data of our users, sugarcane agrarian reform beneficiaries, block farm managers, and Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA) officers.

This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your Personal Information (PI) and Sensitive Personal Information (SPI) in full compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and relevant National Privacy Commission (NPC) issuances.

2 Information We Collect

To provide agricultural tracking, offline sync, and regulatory audit services, HUGPONG collects the following categories of data:

  • Farmer & User Identifiers: Full Name, 11-digit mobile contact number, 8-digit role-prefixed User ID, cooperative association name, and cryptographic password hashes.
  • Field Plot & Agronomic Data: Field Plot ID (e.g. FLD-NCY-001), block farm cluster affiliation, total declared land area in hectares, soil classification, sugarcane variety (e.g., Phil 99-1793, VMC 84-524), and active 6-stage crop cycle progress.
  • Operation & Labor Logs: Field activities performed (land preparation, planting, basal fertilization, weed cultivation, hilling-up, harvesting), itemized input costs, worker headcount, labor vouchers, and timestamps.
  • Audit & Certification Records: SRA inspector validation reports, supervisory edit logs, certification signatures, and verification QR payloads.
  • Diagnostic & Hardware Telemetry: Device hardware model, operating system version, offline storage sync health, battery level, and application version (collected strictly for sync troubleshooting).

3 Lawful Basis & Purposes of Processing

We process your data strictly pursuant to the following lawful grounds under RA 10173 (Sections 12 and 13):

Contractual Necessity & Consent

To verify identity, authenticate farm roles, and maintain sugarcane plot registries across block farm cooperatives.

SRA Regulatory Governance

To enable certified crop reporting, weekly price bulletin broadcasts, and non-repudiable agronomic oversight.

Offline-First Synchronization

To cache operational logs locally on mobile terminals in areas with zero cell signal and synchronize securely upon internet reconnection.

Fraud Prevention & Audit Integrity

To maintain immutable edit audit trails, prevent duplicate expense disbursements, and record security events.

4 Data Storage, Security & Encryption

HUGPONG employs multi-layered technical, physical, and organizational security measures to protect your data:

  • Password Security: Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are protected using Salted SHA-256 cryptographic hashing.
  • Session Integrity: Authentication utilizes rolling, sliding-window session tokens with automated timeout policies.
  • Encrypted Transport: All data transmitted between client devices, the web console, and Google Cloud Firestore is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Zero Third-Party Ad Trackers: HUGPONG contains zero commercial ad trackers, marketing pixels, or third-party data brokers.

5 Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Section 16 of the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you possess the following rights:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know how your agricultural and personal data is collected and processed.
  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of all registered field plots and operation records associated with your User ID.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate crop stage, contact, or hectare allocation data through authorized Farm Manager workflows.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: You may request suspension or removal of your account upon termination of cooperative membership, subject to SRA statutory recordkeeping laws.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at complaints@privacy.gov.ph if your data privacy rights have been violated.
